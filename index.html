01 Institutional Overview

Disciplined advisory for industrial market expansion

Elbaze Traders is an independent industrial real estate brokerage and commercial facilitation firm based in Lahore, Pakistan, established in 2026 under the sole proprietorship of Ali Ahmad. We bridge international manufacturing enterprises and industrial investors with verified private factory sheds, warehouse acreage, and vetted industrial plots across Punjab.

Pakistan represents one of South Asia’s most strategic manufacturing gateways—offering duty-free capital equipment imports, direct deep-water logistics access, and a statutory 10-year federal corporate income tax exemption within gazetted SEZs. We operate with absolute fiduciary transparency, ensuring verified direct landowner asking prices with zero intermediary markups.

03 Capabilities

Curated brokerage & sourcing services

Our engagement scopes are designed to de-risk high-value industrial investments at every phase of market entry.

01 / SITE SEARCH

Industrial Land & Facility Sourcing

Targeted identification of unencumbered industrial parcels, turnkey production facilities, and SEZ plots tailored to your power requirements, logistics access, and tonnage scale.

02 / AUDIT

Title & Infrastructure Due Diligence

Verification of revenue records (Fard, Aks Shajra, Mutation), industrial estate transfer clearances, environmental permits, and sanctioned utility quotas (gas, water, high-tension power).

03 / INSPECTION

On-Ground Site Audits & Video Dossiers

Private executive delegations and remote video surveying. We produce geolocated photo, video, and drone documentation, along with road access and boundary confirmations.

04 / LIAISON

SEZ Authorities & Legal Introductions

Direct liaison with Special Economic Zone management committees, provincial investment boards (PBIT/SBI), corporate attorneys, and chartered accountants for compliant setup.
